Some funds, such as the trust funds in many 401(k) plans, do not publish their holdings in a form we can read. If you know what such a fund holds, add its fund mix here. The fact sheet of the fund lists it.

Loading your holdings…

After a Refresh, each holding that is not looked through shows up here, and so does each holding with a saved mix.

Tick Substitute when you use a similar fund in place of a fund that is not looked through, such as the mutual fund version of a trust fund. The report marks it.

Saved mixes with no matching holding

A holding can get a new name or symbol in Tiller. Link its saved mix to the holding, or delete the mix.
